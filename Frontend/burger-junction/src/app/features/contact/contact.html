Common questions

How long does delivery take?

Most orders reach you in about 30–40 minutes, depending on your location and how busy we are.

What payment methods do you accept?

You can pay by cash on delivery or by UPI at checkout.

How do I track my order?

Open the My Orders page. Your order status updates there as we confirm, prepare and deliver it.

How do I use a coupon?

Copy a code from the Offers page and enter it in the coupon box at checkout before you place your order.

Do you cater parties and events?

Yes. See the Services page for our catering options, then call or email us to enquire.
